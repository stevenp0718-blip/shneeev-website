Free CS2 aim DM warmup server.

AIM DM WARMUP ALWAYS FREE | shneeev is a 24-slot Counter-Strike 2 deathmatch server built for aim practice. Jump in, warm up before your match, and leave whenever you like.

How to join

Open the CS2 console and paste this:

connect 147.135.44.92:27015

Or click here to launch CS2 and connect. You can also find it in the in-game Community Server Browser by searching for "AIM DM WARMUP".

What you get

Rounds rotate automatically through rifles for 15 minutes, pistols for 5 minutes and AWP for 5 minutes, then repeat. Ammo is unlimited and refills on kills, you gain health for every kill, and you can pick your own pistol as a secondary in rifle rounds. Votes are off and cheats are disabled so the server stays fair.
