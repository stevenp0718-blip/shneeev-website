PRIVACY

Your information stays yours.

Effective August 7, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how SHNEEEV handles information through shneeev.com, email review alerts, and the private SHNEEEV Shorts Uploader used to publish SHNEEEV content.

Information collected

If you subscribe to review alerts, we collect the email address you submit. Cloudflare processes security-check and network information to prevent automated abuse. The site also stores your enhanced-effects preference locally in your browser. We do not use that preference to identify you.

How information is used

  • To confirm and deliver review or upload notifications you requested.
  • To protect signup forms from spam and abuse.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website and publishing workflow.

Service providers

SHNEEEV uses Cloudflare for website delivery, security checks, and limited storage; Resend for requested email delivery; YouTube/Google, TikTok, and Meta services for publishing SHNEEEV content. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies.

Publishing integrations

The SHNEEEV Shorts Uploader is a private desktop tool operated by SHNEEEV. Platform authorization tokens are stored locally on the authorized computer and are not published on the website or committed to GitHub. The tool accesses only the permissions needed to upload or manage SHNEEEV-owned content.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the requested service, when required by law, or to protect the security of SHNEEEV and its users.

Retention and choices

Unconfirmed signup records expire automatically. Confirmed subscriber information is retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. You may ignore an unrequested confirmation email, unsubscribe using an email link, or contact us to request access or deletion.

Children

The site and uploader are not directed to children under 13, and SHNEEEV does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to business@shneeev.com.
