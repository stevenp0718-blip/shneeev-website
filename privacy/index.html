Privacy policy.

This explains what information I collect through this website, why I need it, and what choices you have.

Updated September 12, 2026

What this covers

This policy applies to shneeev.com, email review alerts, and the private SHNEEEV Shorts Uploader I use to publish my own content.

What I collect

If you sign up for review alerts, I collect the email address you enter. Cloudflare may process basic network and security information when it checks the signup form for automated abuse. The site also saves your visual-effects preference in your browser, but that preference is not used to identify you.

How I use it

  • To confirm your signup and send the alerts you requested.
  • To protect the signup form and website from spam or abuse.
  • To operate and secure the website and my publishing workflow.

Services involved

I use Cloudflare for website delivery, security checks, and limited storage; Resend to deliver requested emails; and YouTube/Google, TikTok, and Meta services to publish SHNEEEV content. Each company handles information under its own privacy policy.

My publishing tools

The SHNEEEV Shorts Uploader is a private desktop tool that I operate. Authorization tokens are stored locally on my authorized computer. They are not published on this website or committed to GitHub. The tool only requests the platform access needed to upload or manage SHNEEEV-owned content.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell personal information. Information is only shared with services needed to provide what you requested, when required by law, or when necessary to protect this website and its users.

How long information is kept

Unconfirmed signup records expire automatically. A confirmed email address is kept until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it. You can ignore an unwanted confirmation email, use the unsubscribe link in an alert, or contact me to request access or deletion.

Children

This website and uploader are not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email business@shneeev.com.
