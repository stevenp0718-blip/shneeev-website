THE SHORT VERSION

Purpose-built for fingertip grip.

At about 18.9 grams, the Fenrir Pro 8K is built to feel nearly weightless in fingertip grip. Its compact symmetrical shell pairs a PAW3955 sensor with up to 8000 Hz polling, 2.4 GHz wireless, and a configurable virtual sensor position.

The specialized shape will not fit every grip style, but it delivers a focused experience for players who want maximum freedom and minimal weight. The full video covers the hands-on testing and context behind the verdict.
