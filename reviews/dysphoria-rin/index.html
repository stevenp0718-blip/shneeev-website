THE SHORT VERSION

A speed-first glass pad.

Dysphoria positions the Rin for players who prioritize fast movement and fluid tracking. Its 1.5 mm glass construction keeps the profile unusually thin, while the textured coated surface and full-coverage silicone base are intended to deliver consistent movement without the pad shifting on the desk.
